\section{OAuth Client Required Role Assignments}

The OAuth client used by \cxoneflow to communicate with \cxone must be assigned
the roles as described in Table \ref{tab:cxone-oauth-roles}.  The OAuth client
must also be assigned to the tenant authorization scope to allow project visibility
to all tenant projects.

\begin{table}[ht]
    \caption{Required Role Assignments \& Descriptions}  
    \label{tab:cxone-oauth-roles}      
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{cl}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Role} & \textbf{Reason} \\
        \midrule
        \texttt{assign-resource-all-groups} & Allows scan orchestration to assign projects to groups.\\
        \midrule
        \texttt{create-project} & Required for scan orchestration.\\
        \midrule
        \texttt{create-scan} & Required for scan orchestration.\\
        \midrule
        \texttt{manage-reports} & Required for any use of results (feedback, Sarif, etc).\\
        \midrule
        \texttt{update-project} & Required for scan orchestration.\\
        \midrule
        \texttt{view-applications} & Required for scan orchestration.\\
        \midrule
        \texttt{view-policy-management} & Required for \cxone policy integration.\\
        \midrule
        \texttt{view-projects} & Required for scan orchestration.\\
        \midrule
        \texttt{view-project-params} & Required for scan orchestration.\\
        \midrule
        \texttt{view-results} & Required for any use of results (feedback, Sarif, etc).\\
        \midrule
        \texttt{view-scans} & Required for scan orchestration.\\
        \midrule
        \texttt{update-project-params} & Required for scan orchestration.\\
        \midrule
        \texttt{update-scan} & Required for scan orchestration.\\
        \midrule
        \texttt{view-tenant-params} & Required for scan orchestration.\\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabularx}
\end{table}
